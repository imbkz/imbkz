## About

I'm **BiKash**, a Dev at [Interactive Media Pvt Ltd](https://www.interactivemedia.co.in/) in Guwahati. I work on [CAclubindia](https://www.caclubindia.com/) and [LAWyersclubindia](https://www.lawyersclubindia.com/) — two platforms that between them serve over 5 million chartered accountants, tax practitioners, advocates and students across India.

Day to day that means content platforms at real traffic, search across statutes and case law, and the tooling that keeps a community this size running.

> This is my personal account. Opinions here are mine, and nothing on this profile is an official statement from Interactive Media, CAclubindia or LAWyersclubindia.
